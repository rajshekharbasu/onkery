# Onkery

A Motto-like interactive chapter on a phone: you film when asked, strangers' clips appear inside an authored story, and a return visit draws a different mix from the pool. The switch-up is Presence — a someone behind each clip, not a profile.

Working name was Sonder. The piece is Onkery.

## Language

**Participant**:
A person going through the chapter on their phone.
_Avoid_: User, player, viewer, reader

**Clip**:
A short piece of footage a participant films when asked, or a seeded stand-in for one.
_Avoid_: Video, footage, contribution

**Pool**:
Clips silently grouped by Presence and drawn by Motif. This chapter's handful is seeded; a filmed clip may join later as a new Presence for other people, unlabeled.
_Avoid_: Bank, library, dataset, feed

**Prompt**:
What the chapter asks the participant to film.
_Avoid_: Task, challenge, quest, scavenger hunt

**Chapter**:
The one short loop: three Presences, inside/outside chooser, same noticing arc, unnamed you/someone voice that continues from the last Presence's Motif.
_Avoid_: Story, experience, session, novella

**Return**:
Coming back to the chapter so the pool can draw a different mix. Not a branch grown from this participant's past answers.
_Avoid_: Replay, revisit, New Game Plus

**Motif**:
A returning fragment by which a Presence is recognized without being named — hands, a window, an object, a quality of light.
_Avoid_: Tag, identity, signature, character trait

**Line**:
Interior words on a clip, shown as type on video. Not always a confession; light is allowed. Never a diagnosis from the chapter's voice.
_Avoid_: Confession, caption, subtitle, quote

**Opener**:
The first fragment a newcomer is guaranteed — one real person who opted into that slot, light register for this prototype, a clip plus a Line.
_Avoid_: Demo, mascot, tutorial clip, fake person

**Presence**:
One of a handful of someones the participant brushes against more than once, known by a Motif, never by name, unique face, place-name, or bio. The chapter's voice treats their clip as a life and never names their feeling.
_Avoid_: Profile, character, user, ghost
