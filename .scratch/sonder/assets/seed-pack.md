# Sonder — Seed Pack (One Chapter)

Three Presences, seed clips, and the indoor/outdoor prompt arcs. No names, faces, cities, or bios — a Presence is a Motif, recurring.

---

## 1. Presence cards (silent)

### P1

**Motif signature:**
- *Hands* — floured, working a surface (kneading, folding dough)
- *Window* — bottom edge fogged with condensation, one leaf pressed flat against the inside of the glass

**Indoor seed clips**

- Hands
  - (a) Hands pressing into a mound of pale dough on a wooden board, flour dust in the air, wrists only, no face.
  - (b, Return) Same hands folding the dough over itself, knuckles floured, board edge visible, different angle than (a).
- Window
  - (a) A window fogged at the bottom third, one green leaf pressed flat against the inside of the glass.
  - (b, Return) Same window, fog cleared to a smear, the leaf catching a slice of direct light, held a beat longer.

**Outdoor seed clips**

- Hands
  - (a) Hands patting flour off onto denim or an apron, outdoors, fence or brick blurred behind, no face.
  - (b, Return) Hands carrying a cloth-covered bowl outside, floured fingertips gripping the rim, different light than (a).
- Window → Sky (outdoor counterpart)
  - (a) Sky glimpsed through a narrow gap between two buildings, grey-white, a wire cutting across the frame.
  - (b, Return) Same gap between buildings, later light, warmer cast, a bird crossing the frame.

**Never appears:** face, legible text on packaging or containers, any tattoo or engraved jewelry, a kitchen distinct enough to identify a specific home or business, a tiled floor or wall pattern that could geolocate the room.

---

### P2

**Motif signature:**
- *Object* — a worn paperback, spine pale, corner gone soft
- *Light* — bars of light laid across a floor or wall, a low, slatted slant

**Indoor seed clips**

- Object
  - (a) A paperback turned over in one hand, cover angled away from camera, spine worn pale, no title legible.
  - (b, Return) Same book, now open, pages riffled by a thumb, corner of one page turned down, no text readable.
- Light
  - (a) Bars of light laid across a wooden floor through blinds, a shoe or sock-foot crossing through them.
  - (b, Return) Same slatted light now on a wall instead of the floor, later hour, narrower bars.

**Outdoor seed clips**

- Object
  - (a) The same paperback tucked under an arm while a hand shifts a bag strap, blurred street or path behind.
  - (b, Return) Book set down on a bench or ledge, hand resting on top a beat, cover still angled away.
- Light
  - (a) Dappled light moving on pavement or a sleeve, shifting as unseen leaves overhead move in wind.
  - (b, Return) Same dappled light, now on the back of a hand resting on a railing, stiller air.

**Never appears:** face, legible book title or author, visible street signs or house numbers, readable brand logos.

---

### P3

**Motif signature:**
- *Hands* — sorting small objects (screws, buttons, beads) into a tin
- *Window/Sky* — a narrow window near the ceiling, or a sliver of sky low between rooftops, dusk-grey-blue, often crossed by a wire or antenna

**Indoor seed clips**

- Hands
  - (a) Hands sorting small metal objects from a dish into a tin, close on fingers only.
  - (b, Return) Same hands closing the tin lid, one object left on the table beside it, different light than (a).
- Window
  - (a) A narrow window near the ceiling, dusk-blue light coming through, a wire or antenna crossing the pane.
  - (b, Return) Same window, near-dark outside now, a single light reflected in the glass, held a beat longer.

**Outdoor seed clips**

- Hands
  - (a) Hands emptying a small tin into a jacket pocket outdoors, quick, no face, blurred ground below.
  - (b, Return) Hands picking a single object back out of the pocket outdoors, turning it once between fingers.
- Sky
  - (a) A sliver of sky low between rooftops, dusk grey-blue, a wire or antenna crossing the frame.
  - (b, Return) Same low sky, closer to full dark, a bird or plane crossing far off.

**Never appears:** face, house numbers, an identifiable skyline or landmark, coin dates or button brands legible, distinguishing rings.

## 2. Chapter beat sheet — indoor

1. **Onboarding**
   `voice:` "You can film here. Tap, hold, let go — it stops on its own."
   `prompt:` "Whatever's closest to your hand right now."
   `play:` none
   *Notes:* teaches tap-to-film and auto-stop. Keep/redo appears after every recording from here on; skip is always available and the chapter still plays a pool clip if skipped. No Presence yet.

2. **Meet A**
   `voice:` "Someone's hands, mid-morning."
   `prompt:` "Something you're about to put away."
   `play:` P1 / hands / indoor-a
   *Notes:* first meeting with A. The clip shown is never the participant's own footage from beat 1.

3. **Continue from A**
   `voice:` "Someone's window, fogged at the bottom edge."
   `prompt:` "Any surface with something on it — count to two, then let go."
   `play:` P1 / window / indoor-a
   *Notes:* continues from A's own second Motif before the chapter moves on. Not a reply to the participant's clip.

4. **Meet B**
   `voice:` "Someone's object, held then set down."
   `prompt:` "Something you're carrying today, if anything."
   `play:` P2 / object / indoor-a
   *Notes:* introduces B.

5. **Continue from B**
   `voice:` "Someone's light, laid out in bars across a floor."
   `prompt:` "A doorway, any doorway."
   `play:` P2 / light / indoor-a
   *Notes:* continues from B's second Motif.

6. **Meet C**
   `voice:` "Someone's hands, sorting something small."
   `prompt:` "Something small enough to hold in one fist."
   `play:` P3 / hands / indoor-a
   *Notes:* introduces C.

7. **Continue from C**
   `voice:` "Someone's window, high up, near the ceiling."
   `prompt:` "The room's tallest thing."
   `play:` P3 / window / indoor-a
   *Notes:* continues from C's second Motif.

8. **Return to A (second meeting)**
   `voice:` "Someone's hands again. Not the same hour."
   `prompt:` "Whatever's warm right now, if anything is."
   `play:` P1 / hands / indoor-b
   *Notes:* later beat, different clip, same someone.

9. **Leave**
   `voice:` "That's the room, for now."
   `prompt:` none
   `play:` none

## 3. Chapter beat sheet — outdoor

Same arc, same Presence order, different asks and clips.

1. **Onboarding**
   `voice:` "You can film out here too. Tap, hold, let go — it stops itself."
   `prompt:` "Whatever's under your feet right now."
   `play:` none

2. **Meet A**
   `voice:` "Someone's hands, dusted pale."
   `prompt:` "Something you're holding, or about to set down."
   `play:` P1 / hands / outdoor-a

3. **Continue from A**
   `voice:` "Someone's sky, cut into a strip."
   `prompt:` "Look up. Two seconds is enough."
   `play:` P1 / window→sky / outdoor-a

4. **Meet B**
   `voice:` "Someone's object, tucked under an arm."
   `prompt:` "Whatever you brought with you today."
   `play:` P2 / object / outdoor-a

5. **Continue from B**
   `voice:` "Someone's light, moving without them."
   `prompt:` "Any patch of shade nearby."
   `play:` P2 / light / outdoor-a

6. **Meet C**
   `voice:` "Someone's hands, emptying a pocket."
   `prompt:` "Whatever's in your pocket, if anything."
   `play:` P3 / hands / outdoor-a

7. **Continue from C**
   `voice:` "Someone's sky, low between the roofs."
   `prompt:` "The furthest thing you can see from here."
   `play:` P3 / sky / outdoor-a

8. **Return to A (second meeting)**
   `voice:` "Someone's hands again. Different light this time."
   `prompt:` "Something you'd rather not put down yet."
   `play:` P1 / hands / outdoor-b

9. **Leave**
   `voice:` "That's outside, for now."
   `prompt:` none
   `play:` none
