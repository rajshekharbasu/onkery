# What is the one chapter

## Question

What is the authored text and arc of the one short chapter — sonder-themed, not six chapters, still Motto's loop (story, prompts, clips, return)?

Blocked on [What is Presence](02-what-is-presence.md): the chapter has to know what kind of someone it is making room for. HITL.

## Answer

One short chapter, **three** Presences. Motto-style inside/outside chooser, then the **same** arc with two prompt lists.

Start simple: teach tap / keep / redo / skip. Then an unnamed voice speaks to **you** (prompts) and of **someone** (clips). It progresses by continuing from the **last Presence's Motif**, not from what the participant filmed, and not as a chat. Later in the chapter a Presence is met again. Then leave.

Exact prompt lines and seed clip lists are not locked here. Generate them with [presence-prompt-generator.md](../assets/presence-prompt-generator.md).
