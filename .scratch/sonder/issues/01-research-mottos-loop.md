## Answer

Motto on a phone is a mobile-web novella: you read authored text, film short lo-fi clips when asked (or skip), and those clips — yours and strangers' — reappear later inside the story as anonymous stand-ins for the narrator's memories. Six ~15-minute chapters, indoor/outdoor paths, cookies for progress, no accounts, camera as the interface, box over the eyes.
