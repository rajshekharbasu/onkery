# How Motto is the surface

## Question

How much of Motto's chrome does this prototype keep, and where should Presence look like itself?

Cover: vertical mobile web, type-on-video, scavenger-style prompts, "magical" no-tutorial UI, and anything we deliberately drop so a someone can be felt behind a clip. HITL.

## Answer

Keep Motto's disappearing UI: vertical phone web, type on video, teach-by-doing, no help screen. Record chrome is Motto's: tap to film, auto-stop, keep/redo, skip.

Prompts stay commonplace scavenger-asks, chosen so they can yield Motifs — not Motto's square/soft/toes list copied blindly, and not "show me a window" by name.

Clips are shown as clips of a life (type on them). Drop Motto's ghost VFX (mirror/chorus/shaders), the six-chapter menu, and social share. Presence does not get its own look; it lives in Motif plus the chapter's voice.
