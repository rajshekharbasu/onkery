# What is Presence

## Question

What is a Presence — how does the participant sense a person behind a clip without a profile, and without turning this into identity or judgment?

Resolve at least: cardinality (many passing strangers vs one life vs a handful), what you sense, what you never learn, and how this stays no-judgment. HITL; do not stand in for the human.

## Answer

A Presence is one of a handful of someones the participant brushes against more than once in the chapter.

You know them by a Motif — the same hands, window, object, or light — never by a name, unique face, place-name, or bio. The chapter's voice treats the clip as a life in the third person ("someone's window") and never names their feeling. We never ask for a face; if one appears, the eyes are covered. The participant's own clip may join the pool unlabeled, but they are not one of the handful this chapter is teaching them to meet.

No ranking, following, or choosing a favorite. Exact size of the handful is still open.
